# M6 checklist for your iPhone: which pieces join

The steps are also inside the app: tap the small **🧪 Test** tab on the right edge of any screen.

**New:** on your puzzle's page, under Pieces, **🧩 Which pieces join** lists pairs of pieces that probably join, most likely first, with a percentage. Squares of four pieces whose pairs all agree are marked **✓ square**. The first card, **What next**, is the best thing to try now. Each card shows the pieces as photographed and their spots on the box picture, with **📷 Point them out**, **✓ They fit** and **✗ Don't fit**. The live view draws a line between pieces that join (green with ✓ for a square). Tap a piece: its panel now says which pieces join its sides.

**How it works, and why:** I first tested whether the app can tell partners by their shape alone, as planned (the "go/no-go"), on your own 21 pieces from IMG_9276 (24 true joins). It can't: shape put the true partner first for none of the 48 sides (it ranked 19th of about 36 on average), and the colours along the seam only for 4 of 48. Your phone photos are too coarse for the fine differences between Ravensburger's knobs. So, as the plan said, matching is **box-led**: two pieces whose spots on the box picture are next to each other probably join. The percentage is how sure the app is about both spots. Shape only rules out pairs that can't fit.

Allow about 15 minutes. Settings should show the version I sent you.

### Get ready
1. If a banner says a new version is ready, tap **Reload** first, then open this 🧪 Test tab again.
2. Lay your leftover pieces on the table (the ones for your 21 holes), then open *The Kiss* → **Scan pieces** → take an **overview photo**. The pieces under **Pieces** should show their spots among your holes, as in M4.

### Check Which pieces join
1. Under Pieces tap **🧩 Which pieces join**. The first card, **What next**, should be a **square of 4** (four pieces that make a square on the box picture) or a pair.
2. Tap **📷 Point them out**: the camera opens and those pieces pulse, with a line between the ones that join. Find them on your table.
3. Try them together.
   - They fit: tap **✓ All 4 fit** (or **✓ They fit** for a pair). A green note says they're joined, with **Undo**. The next card moves up.
   - A pair doesn't fit: tap **✗ Don't fit** on that pair. It isn't suggested again.
4. Go on with the next few cards. Near the bottom, **✓ N pairs joined** lists what you joined, each with **Take back**.
5. Close the panel and tap a piece with a green dot: under its spot, **Joins (as on the box picture)** shows its partners per side ("on its right · 95 %"), and says when a side's partner hasn't been scanned yet.
6. Open **📷 Live view of my table**: pieces that probably join have a line between them (dashed white with a %, or green ✓ for a square).
7. Tap **📊 How often is the app right?**: a new part **Which pieces join** counts your answers (squares of 4 and other pairs).

### This 🧪 Test tab
1. Can you read these steps and tick them? Close this box and open it again: your ticks should still be there.

### Good to know
- Pairs only appear when the app knows where both pieces go. Pieces whose spot isn't sure (grey or blue) get no pairs yet; a close-up helps.
- Among your holes, the pairs are the holes next to each other, so you'll see them around the group of 11 holes (columns 17–20, rows 7–9) and the 10 holes in the top-right corner (columns 24–28, rows 1–2).
