# M10 checklist for your iPhone: better piece finding

The steps for the newest part are also inside the app: tap the round **🧪** button at the top right of any screen.

## Part 1: touching pieces, the built picture, the nearly finished puzzle

**New:**
- **Touching pieces are split** into one outline per piece on the first photo of a scan, when the group's size is close to a whole number of pieces and every part is piece-shaped. The side along the cut is marked unsure, so its shape isn't trusted there. Groups the app can't split keep the dashed outline with the arrows-apart sign.
- **Single pieces no longer marked "touching"** just because the photo is a little tilted or the piece has big knobs.
- **False "pieces" on the built picture** are mostly gone.
- A group that still touches counts as **about N pieces** by its size: "Found about 58 pieces", and "about N" on the Pieces and Table tabs.
- A photo of your **nearly finished puzzle reads 28 × 36** (it read 28 × 37). Its empty spots are not saved while loose pieces lie on the puzzle, so your progress isn't overcounted.
- Without markers, a slightly tilted first photo is levelled more accurately (the size of each piece's knobs is allowed for).
- Pale outline stretches snap to the thin dark cut line (a small gain).

**Why:** the app review found piece finding good for pieces spread apart (about 97 %), but it lost touching pieces, invented pieces on the built picture and misread your nearly finished puzzle.

**Measured on your photos:** every loose piece on 11 of your photos was counted by hand (479 pieces). Outlined one by one: 252 → 297. False pieces: 43 → 22. No piece lost. IMG_9127: 27 → 49 of 60.

1. Spread about 30 loose pieces, a few touching in twos and threes. Take one photo from straight above. Does "Found about N pieces" match what you laid out?
2. Tap **See photo**. Are most touching pairs and threes outlined one by one? Is any single piece cut in two? (A screenshot helps.)
3. Photograph the nearly finished puzzle with some loose pieces on it: 28 × 36, and the empty spots not saved. Take the pieces off and photograph it again: the empty spots are saved, with red squares on real holes.
4. In both photos, no outlines on the built picture itself.

**Still to do:** pale white-dress pieces are still only partly outlined; pieces in a box lid or the tray pile; the shaded table corner; holes half covered by neighbours' knobs.
