# M8 checklist for your iPhone: tuning on your real puzzle

The steps for the newest part are also inside the app: tap the small **🧪 Test** tab on the right edge of any screen.

## Part 1: colours learned from your pieces

**New:** once **12 pieces are in the puzzle at known spots** (✓ Fits), the app learns how your photos' colours map to the box picture, and places every other piece again. It learns again after every 10 more. The puzzle page then says **"Colours learned from your N pieces in the puzzle"**. It only uses what it learned when it predicts the colours of pieces it didn't learn from clearly better.

**Why:** on the whole box picture (a fresh puzzle), your real pieces were rarely placed right. Your photos' colours under the lamp differ from the box picture in ways the old one-time colour match can't undo. Measured on your 19 real pieces with known spots (each placed with colours learned from the other 18): right spot first **1 → 6**, in the top 5 **3 → 9**.

**When you can check it:** on your next puzzle, after 12 pieces have gone in with ✓ Fits. *The Kiss* uses its holes, where this isn't needed.

1. Start your next puzzle as usual (box picture, scan pieces).
2. Put pieces in and tap **✓ Fits** for each. After the 12th, the line under the piece groups says **"Colours learned from your 12 pieces in the puzzle"**.
3. Do more pieces show Strong or Likely afterwards? Do the suggested spots feel more often right?

## Part 3: faster scans, a lighter live view

**New:** scans are faster (on the Mac your 18 MP photos take 4–7 s for the first photo of a scan and under 2 s for the next ones at about the same height, instead of 7–12 s), and the live view looks at about 7 frames a second while you hold the phone still (25 while moving), to save battery.

1. **Scan pieces** → an overview photo, then another from about the same height. Is the second one faster?
2. **📷 Live view of my table**, phone held still a few seconds: the number after "Tracking ✓" drops to about **7/s**; move it and it goes back up.
3. **Settings → Device check**, then send me the report (Report → Share): your iPhone's own speed with this version.

## Part 2 (next): retuning from your answers

When you've answered ✓ Fits / ✗ Doesn't fit for a good number of pieces (50 or more), send me a backup of the puzzle (puzzle page → Backup → Share backup → save it to Files and put it in the project's `images` folder on the Mac, or AirDrop it). I'll retune the app's percentages and cut-offs from your real answers, on one half, and check them on the other half.
