# M4 (parts 1, 1b, 2, 2b and 3) checklist for your iPhone: where does each piece go?

M4 part 1 suggests, for every scanned piece, **where it goes on the box picture**: the best spot (column and row, counted from the top-left), how sure it is (**Strong**, **Likely**, or **Several spots look alike**) and up to 4 other possible spots. Allow about 20 minutes. Screenshots of anything wrong help.

## Part A: update
1. Open Puzzle Lens from your Home Screen. If a banner says an update is ready, tap it.
2. **Settings**: the version at the bottom should match the one I sent you.

## Part B: check the box picture
1. Open *The Kiss*. The box picture at the top must show **exactly the picture**, edge to edge (no lid border, no white margin). If it doesn't, redo the box picture step (the 4 dots on the picture's corners).

## Part C: scan and see the spots
1. Pieces scanned before this version have no spot yet ("not placed yet"). Tap **Delete scanned pieces… → Delete and scan again**, or use **🖼 Choose a photo** with a photo of loose pieces.
2. Back on the puzzle screen, under Pieces you'll see "On the box picture: N strong · N likely · N several spots look alike" (it may first say "Finding spots…").
3. **Tap a piece**: it shows "Column X, row Y (Strong, 93 %)", other possible spots, and the box picture with the spot in **green** (others yellow, dashed).
4. Check a few: does the piece's picture match the green spot? A green dot on a thumbnail = Strong, yellow = Likely.

## Part D: the real test (needs the finished puzzle)
This tells us exactly how well it works on your pieces:
1. With the puzzle finished, **take out about 10 pieces** from different areas (some dark, some light, some plain wall, 1–2 edge pieces). Leave the holes.
2. Take an **overview of the puzzle with the holes**: the finished-puzzle check lists the gaps ("column X, row Y") and shows red squares. Screenshot it.
3. Spread the 10 pieces on the table, scan them (overview, then close-ups if offered).
4. Tap each piece: is its green spot one of the holes?
5. **Export backup** and put it in the images folder on your Mac (it holds your box picture, so I can measure everything here).

## Part E: after the part 1b fixes (your 21 pieces)
1. Scan your 21-piece photo again (**🖼 Choose a photo** → IMG_9276). The pale pieces' outlines should now follow their real edge.
2. Under Pieces, most should now say **Several spots look alike** (honest), a few **Strong**. Tap a Strong one: its green spot should be one of the holes (top-right corner, or around the faces).
3. Scan the holes photo (IMG_9280): the finished-puzzle check should list **21 gaps**, with red squares exactly on the holes.

## Part F: leftover pieces into the empty spots (part 2)
✅ Checked on your iPhone 2026-11-09.

With most of the puzzle done, the app now uses your photo of the puzzle with its holes: the leftover pieces are matched **only to the empty spots**, and **each spot takes one piece**.
1. Update the app (Part A). Settings should show the version I sent you.
2. Open *The Kiss* → **📷 Scan pieces** → **🖼 Choose a photo** → your holes photo (IMG_9280). It doesn't matter whether the screen says "Overview" or "Close-ups". It should say **21 possible gaps** and then **"Saved: your leftover pieces will now be matched only to these 21 empty spots"**. If it says "Close-up read: … pieces" instead, the app hasn't updated yet: close it fully and open it again, then check the version.
3. If your Pieces list has pieces other than the 21 leftovers (from earlier tests), tap **Delete scanned pieces… → Delete and scan again** and choose your 21-piece photo (IMG_9276). Otherwise just go back to the puzzle page.
4. Under Pieces you should see a green box: **"Leftover pieces are matched only to the 21 empty spots…"**, then a count such as "9 strong · 2 likely · 8 several spots look alike · 1 fit none of the empty spots".
5. **Tap a piece:** the box picture shows the 21 empty spots as light squares, its spot in **green**, other possible spots in yellow. Check the **Strong** ones against the real holes: they should all be right.
6. "Fit none of the empty spots" is for things that aren't pieces (the bottle's label, a patch of table) or pieces from somewhere else.
7. Optional: **Use the whole picture instead** goes back to searching the whole picture; choosing the holes photo again brings the empty spots back. After putting pieces in, photograph the puzzle again so the empty spots are updated.

## Part G: knob shapes (part 3)
✅ Checked on your iPhone 2026-11-09: the top-right wall pieces land in their holes.

1. Update the app (Part A). Settings should show the version I sent you.
2. Open *The Kiss*. Under Pieces you'll see **"Knob shapes not used yet"**: your holes and pieces were read before this version.
3. **📷 Scan pieces → 🖼 Choose a photo →** your holes photo (IMG_9280). It should say "21 possible gaps … Saved".
4. Go back. **Delete scanned pieces… → Delete and scan again → 🖼 Choose a photo →** your 21-piece photo (IMG_9276).
5. Go back to the puzzle page. The "Knob shapes not used yet" line should be gone. Expect about **17 strong · 1 likely · 2 several · 3 fit none**.
6. Tap the pieces from the **top-right corner** (the plain wall ones): their green squares should now be spread over the 10 corner holes, each where its knobs fit.
