# Milestone 1: your iPhone checklist

This checks the M1 "skeleton" on your real iPhone. Nothing counts as working until you've done this.
It takes about 20–30 minutes. Write down anything that looks wrong, even small things.

**You need:** your iPhone, your Mac (for AirDrop), and your puzzle box (or any detailed picture).

---

## Part A: Open the app in Safari

1. On the iPhone, open **Safari** and go to: **https://zivkogodzirov.github.io/puzzle-lens/**
2. You should see the **Puzzle Lens** home page with a dark background and tiles (Live camera, Device check, Send report, Setup guide, Settings).

## Part B: Device check in the Safari tab

1. Tap **Device check**.
2. **Step 1:** tap **Run checks**. Wait until it finishes (about half a minute).
3. **Step 2:** tap the camera tests button. When asked, tap **Allow** for the camera.
   - Point the phone at your puzzle box (something with lots of detail), about 30–40 cm away, and keep it still.
   - It takes about a minute and takes about 11 photos. The torch will flash. That's expected.
4. **Step 3:** tap the iPhone camera screen button. Your iPhone's own camera opens: take a photo of the box and tap **Use Photo**.
5. **Step 4 (optional, memory test):** this pushes memory until iOS stops it, so the app may close by itself. If it does, open it again and go back to **Device check** to see the result.
6. The top of the screen should now say **All done ✓**.
7. Go to **Send report** → tap **Prepare report** → tap **Share report** → choose **AirDrop** → your Mac. (Or Mail it to yourself.)

## Part C: Install it on your Home Screen, and repeat

1. In Safari, tap the **Share** button. On iOS 26/27 it may be behind the **⋯** button.
2. Tap **Add to Home Screen**. It may be under **View More**. If you see **Open as Web App**, turn it on. Tap **Add**.
3. Close Safari and open **Puzzle Lens** from its Home Screen icon.
4. Do **Part B again** inside this Home Screen app. Its results are kept separately from Safari's, so it needs its own run and its own report.

## Part D: Live camera and the test marker

1. On your **Mac**, open **https://zivkogodzirov.github.io/puzzle-lens/#/help** and click **Show test marker**. A black-and-white square fills the screen. (Or print the marker page from the same screen.)
2. On the **iPhone** (Home Screen app): tap **Live camera** → **Start camera**, and point it at the marker on the Mac screen from about 40 cm.
3. ✅ Expected: a **green outline with "#0"** sits on the marker within about a second, and follows it when you move the phone.
4. Turn the phone: **upright**, **sideways to the left**, **sideways to the right**. Does the green outline stay on the marker each time? Note any position where it is in the wrong place.
5. The small text box at the top left is the **performance display**. Note the **tracker** number (frames per second) and the **gap** number.
6. Tap the big round **Snap** button. A "Last photo" panel slides up. Note the **Size** (expected 4032 × 3024) and **Camera paused**.
7. Tap **2×**, then **Snap** again, and note the same two values.
8. Tap **‹ Home**. The green camera dot at the top of the iPhone screen should disappear within a couple of seconds.
9. Open **Live camera** again, start the camera, then **lock the phone** for 10 seconds and unlock it. The camera should come back by itself, or show **Resume camera**.

## Part E: Without internet

1. Turn on **Airplane Mode**.
2. Open Puzzle Lens from the Home Screen. It should open normally.
3. **Live camera** should still find the marker.
4. Turn Airplane Mode off again.

## What to send me

1. **Two reports:** one from the Safari tab and one from the Home Screen app. AirDrop puts them in your Mac's **Downloads** folder; just tell me they're there.
2. **A photo of your puzzle box** (front, straight on, in good light) and **a photo of your mat** (empty, from above). AirDrop these to the Mac too.
3. **Your notes:** anything that looked wrong, was confusing, or was slow. Part D step 4 is especially useful.
4. **Answers to the open questions:**
   - Do you have, or will you get, an **overhead phone stand** (holding the phone about 60 cm above the table)?
   - Your puzzle's **brand**, **piece count**, and the **finished size** printed on the box?
   - Your **mat** size and colour, your **table** size, and do you have an **A4 printer**?

## Known limits in this version

- The **2× lens test** shows a measured number but doesn't say yes or no yet. I'll set that from your report.
- The **Home Screen app asks for camera permission every time it opens**. That's an iOS bug, not something you did.
- **Private Browsing:** photos can't be kept for reports. The app says so with a gentle note.
