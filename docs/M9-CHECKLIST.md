# M9 checklist for your iPhone: a professional design

The steps for the newest part are also inside the app: tap the round **🧪** button at the top right of any screen.

The design work follows the app review's plan, in 6 stages, each checked on your iPhone before the next: 1. readable now; 2. foundation (one set of colours, buttons, lists and pop-up cards, one icon set); 3. the core loop (a piece card from the bottom with It fits / Not there and Undo, clearer outline patterns); 4. four tabs inside each puzzle (Table · Picture · Pieces · More); 5. a scan camera laid out like the iPhone Camera app; 6. polish (cut-out piece tiles, text that follows the iPhone Text Size, VoiceOver).

## Part 1: readable now

**New:**
- Colours that are easy to read in light mode. 31 text styles were too faint; every pair of colours is now measured (text at least 4.5:1, lines and dots at least 3:1) in light and dark mode.
- ✓ Fits / ✗ Doesn't fit are big (56 points) and side by side everywhere; small buttons are at least 44 points.
- Every piece outline on a photo or in the live view has a dark edge, so it shows on your cream table (measured on your photos: the outlines stood out from the table at only 1.0–2.9 before, the dark edge now at about 5).
- **Undo** for 10 seconds after an answer in the live view.
- A **bar at the bottom of the puzzle page**: Scan · Live view · Fill a spot · Joins.
- The app reopens your last puzzle. The set-up list folds to "✓ Set-up finished". The screen stays on while a puzzle page is open.
- Pinch-zoom works outside the camera screens. The Home Screen app stays upright.
- The 🧪 button is in the top bar. **Tester mode** (Settings) hides the developer tools and numbers when you switch it off; it starts on.
- "1 piece" instead of "1 pieces", and plain sentences instead of computer error text.
- Reduce Motion is respected.

**Why:** the review found the app's engine ahead of its screens: the main buttons were 2–3 screens down, many colours were too faint in light mode, and outlines vanished on a cream table.

1. In light mode, open the app from the Home Screen: it opens on *The Kiss*. Is everything easy to read?
2. Use the bottom bar on the puzzle page. Does anything get covered by it?
3. Tap a piece: big ✓ Fits / ✗ Doesn't fit side by side. Then in **Live view**, answer one and tap **Undo**.
4. Are the outlines in the live view easy to see on your table? Does it still feel smooth?
5. Settings → Tester mode Off: the tools and numbers hide; set it back On.
6. Leave a puzzle page open past your Auto-Lock time: the screen stays on.

**Only your iPhone can tell:** the status bar in both modes, whether the app stays upright, whether pinch-zoom causes accidental zooms, whether the screen stays on, and whether the live view stays smooth with the dark edges.

## Part 2: foundation (one look across the app)

**New:**
- One set of building blocks used everywhere: an iPhone-style top bar (**‹ back** goes to the screen you came from, not always Home), lists grouped like iPhone Settings, coloured message boxes with an icon, a sheet that slides up from the bottom, an iPhone-style confirmation for big deletions (delete a puzzle, delete scanned pieces, erase everything), and a dark **Undo** message above the bottom bar after ✓ Fits and Joins answers.
- One set of 42 matching line icons (Lucide, built into the app so it works offline) replaces the emoji.
- Home: a big title, your puzzles as a list with their box pictures, one **New puzzle** button, the testing tools in a "Diagnostics" group.
- ✓ Fits is a solid green button; Delete buttons are red words on a light red background.
- Every colour in the code is now a named colour with a light and dark value (a test stops raw colours from coming back).

**Why:** the review found the screens looked like a test build: every screen built its own boxes and buttons, 23 emoji did the job of icons, and "‹ Home" was the only way back.

1. Look at Home, Settings, a puzzle page, Progress and the camera screens in light and dark mode. Does it feel like one app?
2. Tap ✓ Fits on a piece, then **Undo** in the dark message at the bottom.
3. Try the delete confirmations (Delete scanned pieces…, Delete this puzzle…, Erase all data) and tap **Cancel** each time.
4. With Tester mode on: Settings → Components (for testing) shows every building block.

## Part 3: the core loop (tap a piece, answer, undo)

**New:**
- Tapping a piece opens a **card from the bottom**: its picture, "Column · Row", how sure the app is in words, and a **close-up of the box picture** with the spot ringed (the whole picture one tap away).
- Two big answers, **Not there** and **It fits**, each with an **Undo**. "Not there" moves straight to the next place to try. "Also possible" shows the other places as small close-ups.
- **"Not a piece"** is one tap with Undo (the removed thing is kept aside, so Undo puts it back exactly).
- New words everywhere: "It fits / Not there" ("They fit" for pairs), "Very likely / Maybe / Several places look alike"; percentages only in Tester mode.
- Outlines: **solid = fine, dashed = have a look**, always with a badge saying why (? faint, arrows apart touching, moved?), a cross on things that aren't pieces; one label per piece, never overlapping; a square of 4 as one shape with one label; a **?** legend; a **Pieces in view** list.

**Why:** the review found the panel opened off screen with a spot 12 points wide, two-tap confirmations, and outline colours and dashes that meant different things on different screens.

1. Tap a piece: does the close-up help you find the spot? Answer with It fits / Not there and try Undo.
2. In the live view: are the labels and badges clear on your table? Try the **?** and **Pieces in view**.
3. Tap **Not a piece** on something and then **Undo**.

## Part 4: structure (four tabs in each puzzle)

**New:**
- Each puzzle has four tabs at the bottom instead of one long page:
  - **Table**: your last table photo right in the tab with its labels, a status line and a progress ring, **Start live view**, and a "Next step" bar for pieces that join.
  - **Picture**: a progress ring, the box picture as a zoomable map (bright = in, dim = to find, blue circle = its piece is on your table), tap a spot to see its pieces and answer; "Photograph the puzzle so far".
  - **Pieces**: All · Border · Inside · Check, pieces 4 across, "Which pieces join" and "Sort into trays" as pages of their own.
  - **More**: backup (the reminder is now a red dot and a once-a-day message), new lot of pieces, scanned pieces, puzzle details with rename, Delete this puzzle.
- **Scan** is the camera button at the top of a puzzle. ‹ Home goes straight to your puzzles.
- **Home**: each puzzle as a card with its picture and "N of M in place".

**Why:** the review found the main actions 2–3 screens down a very long page, and panels opening inside it.

1. Open your puzzle: it opens on **Table**. Try each tab. Is anything you used before hard to find?
2. On **Picture**, zoom the box picture and tap a spot with a blue circle.
3. On **Pieces**, try the four filters and open Which pieces join, then ‹ Pieces.
4. On **More**, check your backup and tap Cancel on Delete this puzzle.
