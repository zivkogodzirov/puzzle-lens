# M2 checklist for your iPhone

M2 adds: your puzzle's set-up, the printed markers, finding pieces on your table, and the first catalog.
Do this in the **Home Screen app** (your puzzle stays there). Allow about 45 minutes.
After each part, note anything that looked wrong. A screenshot helps.

## Part A: update
1. Open the Puzzle Lens app from your Home Screen.
2. If a banner says an update is ready, tap it.
3. Open **Settings** and check the version at the bottom. It should match the one I sent you.

## Part B: new puzzle
1. On Home, tap **New puzzle**.
2. Name: *The Kiss*. Pieces: tap **1000**.
3. **Box photo:**
   1. Tap **Take a photo of the box**.
   2. Photograph the lid straight from above, filling most of the screen.
   3. Drag the 4 dots onto the corners of the *picture* (not the lid edge, if the lid has a border).
   4. Tap **Straighten the picture**. Check it looks right and the right way up; use **↻ Turn picture** if not.
4. **Grid:** it should suggest **28 across × 36 down = 1008**. Tap **Next**.
5. **Print markers:**
   1. Tap **Print marker sheets**, then print both A4 pages at **100 % / actual size** (from the iPhone, or **Send to my Mac** and print there).
   2. Measure the black bar on a printed sheet with a ruler and answer the question.

## Part C: lay out the markers (step 5)
1. Cut out the 8 cards along the dashed lines.
2. On your table, clear an area about **70 × 50 cm** and lay the cards around it as the picture shows: #0 at the far-left corner, then clockwise. Arrows point away from you.
3. Light from the side if you can: a window or a lamp off to one side, not straight above.
4. Tap **Open the camera**. Stand at the near side and hold the phone flat over the middle, high enough to see all 8 cards (about 65–75 cm).
5. The photo takes itself when all 8 are in view and the phone is still. You should see **All 8 markers measured ✓**.
6. Tap **Finish set-up**.

Tell me: how easy was it to get all 8 cards in view? Did the photo take itself?

## Part D: first scan (about 50 pieces)
1. Spread about **50 pieces** face up inside the cards, with small gaps between them. **Include 5–10 of the palest pieces** (the light parts of the dress), and push two pieces together on purpose.
2. On the puzzle screen, tap **📷 Scan pieces**.
3. **Overview:**
   1. Hold the phone flat over the middle with all 8 cards in view, like before.
   2. The gauge shows a bubble (green = flat) and fills a ring while you hold still, then the photo takes itself.
   3. Tip the phone a little to one side: the bubble should move towards the side you lifted, like a spirit level. Tell me if it goes the other way.
   4. It says "Found N pieces".
4. Check:
   - Is N close to the number you laid out?
   - Grey outlines should sit on the pieces in the live view and stay on them as you move the phone.
   - Touching pieces should have **orange dashes**.
   - Pale pieces may have **yellow** outlines ("check this one"), or may be missing.
5. **Close-ups:**
   1. The screen switches to **Close-ups 2×**.
   2. Follow the arrow and the instructions (lower / flat / hold still). Each close-up takes itself.
   3. If it offers **Use the tilt sensor too**, tap it and allow motion access.
   4. Keep going until it says all close-ups are done, or you've taken about 6.
6. Tap **‹ Back**. The puzzle screen shows thumbnails of the pieces found and a **measured piece size**. It should be close to 18 × 19 mm, and the finished size close to 50 × 70 cm.

Tell me:
- How many pieces did you lay out, and how many did it find?
- Which ones were missed (pale? touching?)
- How long did each photo take to process?

## Part E: send me the results
1. On the puzzle screen, tap **Export backup**, then **Share backup**, and AirDrop it to your Mac. Put the `.pfbackup` file in the `images` folder.
   - It holds the full photos of your last 10 scans plus the catalog, so I can measure exactly what was missed.
2. Also send a **report** (Report screen) and put it in the `images` folder.
3. Tell me "M2 results in images", with your notes from Parts C and D.

## Known limits in this version
- **Pale pieces on your white table** are the hardest case. On computer-made test photos the app finds about 94–97 % of all pieces, but far fewer of the very palest. Your photos will give the real number.
- A close-up that shows **no marker** (the very middle of the table) can't be placed yet; the app says so and adds nothing from it. Placing it from the pieces comes in a later update.
- **Moved pieces:** if you move pieces between photos, the app may count a moved piece twice. Recognising moved pieces comes in M5.
- Touching pieces are flagged, not split.
