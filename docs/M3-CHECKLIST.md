# M3 checklist for your iPhone: shapes, Border mode, finished-puzzle check

M3 (first part) reads each piece's **shape** (4 corners; each side flat, tab or blank), sorts pieces into **corner / edge / inside**, and adds a **finished-puzzle check** that counts the pieces of a completed puzzle and finds gaps. Allow about 20 minutes. A screenshot of anything that looks wrong helps.

## Part A: update
1. Open Puzzle Lens from your Home Screen. If a banner says an update is ready, tap it.
2. **Settings**: the version at the bottom should match the one I sent you.

## Part B: Border mode (loose pieces)
1. Spread **about 20 pieces face up**, with small gaps, and include **some edge pieces and at least one corner** (you know which they are).
2. On the puzzle screen: **Delete scanned pieces… → Delete and scan again**. Take the overview, then 2–3 close-ups (2×) if offered.
3. Back on the puzzle screen, under Pieces, tap the buttons **All / Border / Inside / Not sure**:
   - **Border**: corners have an **orange** frame, edges a **teal** frame; the line above says e.g. "Corners 1 of 4 · Edges 6 of 120".
   - Check: is every corner/edge piece you laid out in Border? Is anything in Border that is not an edge piece?
   - **Not sure** holds pieces whose shape wasn't clear (touching pieces, pale outlines). A close-up usually helps.
4. Note how many were right / wrong / not sure.

## Part C: face down (your idea)
1. Turn the same pieces **face down** (blue back up) and repeat Part B step 2–3.
2. Note whether Border finds the same pieces. (Shape reading works on the outline only, so the back side should work the same; the photos tell us.)

## Part D: finished-puzzle check
1. With the puzzle **finished**, take an **overview** from straight above with the whole puzzle and some table around it in view, the phone held flat.
2. Expected: a message "**Finished puzzle: 28 across × 36 down = 1,008 pieces**, counted from the seams. That matches your puzzle. No gaps found." Nothing is added to your pieces.
3. Take **one piece out** (leave the hole) and take the overview again. Expected: "1 possible gap … column X, row Y", and a **red square** on that spot when you tap **See the last photo**.

## Known limits
- Shape reading: on computer-made test photos the piece type is right for ~96 % of clean pieces (overview and close-up); reads it trusts are right ~97 % of the time. A blank whose hollow is filled by a dark shadow can look flat (a piece wrongly in Border): close-ups help.
- The finished check needs the seams visible: a sharp overview from straight above. Bright patches of print that look like the table are checked for texture, but a very plain light area could be mistaken for a gap.
- Shape-only solving (your back-side idea) is **not** in this step: see the go/no-go below.

## Shape-only go/no-go (when you have time)
1. Put together a block of about **7 × 7 pieces**, photograph it from above (overview + close-ups).
2. Break it up, spread the 49 pieces (face down is fine) and scan them (overview + close-ups).
3. Send me the backup. I measure how often shape alone finds each piece's true neighbour on **your** pieces.
