# M9, part 1: easier to read and use (and M8 parts 1, 3 and 4, M7, M6, M5, still to check)

**New in M9 part 1** (the first of 6 design stages): colours that are easy to read in light mode, bigger ✓ Fits / ✗ Doesn't fit buttons side by side, a dark edge under every piece outline so they show on your cream table, **Undo** in the live view, a **bar at the bottom of the puzzle page** (Scan · Live view · Fill a spot · Joins), the app reopening your last puzzle, the 🧪 button in the top bar, and a **Tester mode** switch in Settings (on for now, so nothing you test with disappears).

**Button names in the older steps below:** the puzzle page's big buttons moved into the bottom bar. "Scan pieces" is now **Scan**, "📷 Live view of my table" is **Live view**, "🎯 Fill this spot" is **Fill a spot**, and "🧩 Which pieces join" is **Joins**.

**New in M8 part 4:** the fixes from the app review. Bugs: the note after a scan photo no longer covers the gauge, a photo of the finished puzzle now says how many loose pieces lie beside it, a misread "finished" count no longer overwrites your saved empty spots, and a tapped piece's panel no longer jumps. Speed (on the Mac, with your own photos): scan photos take about a fifth less time, the first photo after **Start a new batch** no longer freezes for 2 seconds, working out where pieces go is 3–6 times faster, and trays sort in about 0.05 s instead of half a second. Each change was checked against the version before it by a second, independent check, and your 41 photos give the same pieces as before.

**New in M8 part 1:** once **12 pieces are in the puzzle at known spots** (✓ Fits), the app learns how your photos' colours map to the box picture and places every other piece again; the puzzle page says **"Colours learned from your N pieces in the puzzle"**. This is for a fresh puzzle on the whole picture (among the holes of a nearly finished puzzle it isn't needed). Measured on your 19 real pieces: right spot first **1 → 6**, in the top 5 **3 → 9**.

**New in the M8 part 3 follow-up:** a piece outlined much smaller than the others on a photo (usually a pale piece on your cream table) is marked **yellow, "only partly outlined"**, and its shape isn't used, only its picture. Your photo IMG_9127 has about 60 loose pieces (not 95, as I wrote first); all were found: 27 alone, about 29 in groups of touching pieces (spread them apart), 1 pale one only in part.

**New in M8 part 3:** scans are faster (on the Mac your 18 MP photos now take 4–7 s for the first photo of a scan and under 2 s for the next ones at about the same height, instead of 7–12 s), and the live view looks at fewer frames while you hold the phone still, to save battery.

**Nothing to test for it on *The Kiss*:** it uses its holes, where the colours come from the holes already. You'll see it on your next puzzle, after 12 pieces have gone in with ✓ Fits. The steps below are the ones still open from M7, M6 and M5.

## M9 part 1: easier to read and use
Do these in **light mode** first (Settings › Display & Brightness › Light), then once more in dark mode.

### Opening the app
1. Close Puzzle Lens fully (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it from the Home Screen. It opens straight on *The Kiss*. **‹ Home** takes you to your puzzles.
2. At the top right of every screen there is a round **🧪** button (with a red dot when there are new steps). Tap it: these steps open. The strip on the right edge is gone.
3. Turn the phone sideways: the Home Screen app stays upright. (If it still turns, remove the app from the Home Screen and add it again, then try once more.)
4. Look at the time and battery at the top in light and dark mode: easy to read in both? On the camera screens (Scan, Live view) in light mode there may now be a light strip above the black camera picture: does it bother you?

### The puzzle page
1. At the bottom there is a bar: **Scan**, **Live view**, **Fill a spot**, **Joins** (with the number of pairs as a small blue badge). It stays while you scroll. Scroll to the very end: **Delete this puzzle…** is fully above the bar.
2. Set-up is done, so instead of the list of steps there is one line: **✓ Set-up finished**.
3. Above the pieces, **All / Border / Inside** (and the others) look like one control; the one you chose is raised and bold.
4. The line "● found · ● shapes read · ● placed on box" is easy to read; the middle dot is now **purple**.
5. Tap a piece: **✓ Fits** and **✗ Doesn't fit** are big buttons side by side, fully above the bar. Words like "strong" and "likely" are easy to read.
6. Tap **Fill a spot** in the bar: the panel opens with a **Close** button. The pieces offered show 2 across, each with **✓ Fits** and **✗ No**. Close it. Do the same with **Joins**.
7. Leave the puzzle page open without touching the phone for longer than your Auto-Lock time: the screen stays on. On Home it locks as usual.
8. Pinch to zoom the text, then pinch back. Double-tapping a button doesn't zoom.

### Outlines and the live view
1. Tap **Live view** and point the phone at your pieces on the cream table. Every outline has a **dark edge**, so it's easy to see. Pieces whose shape was read are light violet now.
2. Tap a piece with a spot, then **✓ Fits**. A note at the bottom has an **Undo** button that stays about 10 seconds. Tap **Undo**: "Undone: the piece is back on the table." (If you close the live view first, the puzzle page shows the note with its Undo.)
3. Tap the same piece, **✗ Doesn't fit**, then **Undo**: "Undone: that spot is offered again."
4. Pinching or double-tapping in the live view and on the Scan camera doesn't zoom the screen.
5. Close it and open **Show my table**, and the last scan photo (**Scan** → **See the last photo**): the outlines there have the dark edge too.

### Tester mode and words
1. Open **Settings**: **Tester mode** at the top is **On**. Tap **Off**, go Home: Live camera, Device check, Send report, Activity log and the 🧪 button are gone. Open your puzzle → **Scan**: the "cm high · tilt" line under the gauge is gone; in **Live view** there's no "/s" after "Tracking ✓".
2. Set Tester mode back to **On** (please keep it on while we test): everything is back. On the **Live camera** screen the speed display (HUD) now starts hidden: tap **HUD** to show it.
3. If you ever see "1 pieces" or a message that looks like computer text ("undefined", "TypeError", words joined together), please send a screenshot.

### Reduce Motion (optional)
1. Settings › Accessibility › Motion › **Reduce Motion** on. In **Joins**, tap **📷 Point them out**: the rings around the pieces stand still (thick) instead of pulsing. Turn it off again if you like.

## M8 part 4: fixes from the app review (bugs and speed)

### The note after a scan photo
1. Open *The Kiss* → **Scan pieces**. If asked, tap **Allow the tilt sensor**, so the round gauge shows at the top.
2. Take an overview photo of your loose pieces. The note with the result now sits **in the panel at the bottom**, above **Choose a photo**. It no longer covers the gauge's words at the top. Can you read its first lines without scrolling?
3. If the note is long, a small blue **More ▾** shows on it: tap it, and the whole note opens over the camera. Tap **Close**: the note is still there. Tap the note itself: it goes away.
4. With a note showing, turn the phone sideways: the note moves to a column on the left and isn't cut off. Turn it back.
5. With a note showing, tap **Choose a photo**, then **Cancel**. If **Resume camera** appears, it sits near the top with the note below it, and both can be tapped.
6. Try steps 2–3 once in **Safari** too (not the Home Screen app). The screen is shorter there: the first lines must still show, with **More ▾** if there is more.

### A photo of the nearly finished puzzle
1. **Scan pieces** → **Choose a photo** → a photo of the nearly finished puzzle with some loose pieces beside it (like your IMG_9280).
2. The green note: **"Finished puzzle: 28 across × 36 down…"**, **"That matches your puzzle…"**, then straight away **"Saved… 21 empty spots…"** and the new line **"About N loose pieces lie next to the puzzle…"** (they used to be left out without a word). Is N close to the loose pieces you see? (On IMG_9280 the app says 8, and I count 8.) The long list of gaps comes after.
3. This note is long, so while it shows, the gauge gets smaller (no "cm high · tilt" line) and **Coin for true size** hides. Tap the note away: both come back. A short note (like "Hardly any pieces…") keeps the full gauge and the coin choice.
4. If a photo of the puzzle says **"Your puzzle is set to 28 × 36, so nothing was saved from this photo"**, the app counted the rows or columns wrong (your IMG_9244 reads 28 × 37). Your saved empty spots stay as they were. Take the photo again from straight above.

### The puzzle page
1. Scroll down to the small pictures of your pieces and tap one. Its panel comes into view **in one move**, and nothing jumps a moment later. Tap **✓ Fits** straight away: it counts once (**✓ 1 in the puzzle** goes up by one), and "Remove it?" never opens by mistake. Then tap **Undo**.
2. Tap a piece on **Show my table** or in **📷 Live view of my table**, then **Show where it goes**: its panel is on screen at once.
3. The set-up list shows **4 steps** for your puzzle (no printing): **"Step 4 of 4 · Ready to scan"**.
4. Under the box picture: **"White outlines: the 21 empty spots."**
5. **Sort into trays**: the trays show almost at once. Are the tray labels right? Put a piece back from a tray: does it show with the right colour group?
6. **Delete scan**, then **Restore**: the Bin card appears, then goes. Close the app fully and open the puzzle again: the same as before you closed it.
7. Only when you do M7 part 1 (a new batch, last): the first photo after **Start a new batch** comes back without the 2-second freeze.

## M6: which pieces join (still to check)
1. Under Pieces tap **🧩 Which pieces join**. The first card, **What next**, should be a **square of 4** (or a pair), with its spots marked on a small piece of the box picture.
2. Tap **📷 Point them out**: the camera opens and those pieces pulse, with a line between the ones that join. Find them on your table.
3. Try them together.
   - They fit: tap **✓ All 4 fit** (or **✓ They fit** for a pair). A green note says they're joined, with **Undo**, and the next card moves up.
   - A pair doesn't fit: tap **✗ Don't fit** on that pair. It isn't suggested again.
4. Go on with a few more cards. Near the bottom, **✓ N pairs joined** lists what you joined, each with **Take back**.
5. Close the panel and tap a piece with a green dot: under its spot, **Joins (as on the box picture)** shows its partners ("on its right · 95 %"), and which sides are next to the done part of the puzzle or have a partner not scanned yet.
6. Open **📷 Live view of my table**: pieces that probably join have a line between them (dashed white with a %, or green ✓ for a square of 4).
7. Tap **📊 How often is the app right?**: a new part **Which pieces join** counts your answers.

## Still to check from M5
**M5 in short:** the 📷 live view of your table (part 1), ✓ Fits / ✗ Doesn't fit with the 📊 page (part 2), rescans that don't count a piece twice (part 3), and a smoother live view that notices moved pieces, plus Quick scans (part 4).

### M5 part 4: a smoother live view, and Quick scans
1. Open **📷 Live view of my table** and move the phone steadily across the table: the outlines should keep up with the pieces instead of trailing behind.
2. Hold the phone still: the outlines should sit calmly on the pieces (no jitter).
3. With the live view open, take **2 pieces** off the table: within a second or two their outlines turn **red and dashed** ("moved?" on bigger pieces), and the top line says **"2 not where they were: scan again to update"**. Put them back where they were: the outlines turn normal again.
4. Open **Settings → Scan mode** and choose **Quick**. Scan your pieces: after one overview photo the note says **"Quick scan done"** and the camera stays on overviews. Set it back to **Full** afterwards (or keep Quick if you like it).

### M5 part 3: rescans and moved pieces
1. With your pieces scanned (as above), take another **overview photo** from a different place (Scan pieces). The note should say **"Found N pieces (0 new)"** (the coin and other things on the table aren't pieces).
2. Move **3 pieces** by hand to other places on the table (turn one of them round), then take another overview photo. The note should say **"3 pieces were moved: recognised, not counted again"**, or some of them become **pieces to check**.
3. If the puzzle page shows **"Pieces to check"**: tap **Check them**. Each row shows the piece as seen now and the pieces it may be: tap **This one** under the right one (or **A different piece**). The **?** goes away.
4. Take **2 pieces** off the table and photograph again: the note says **"2 pieces are no longer where they were"**. Put them back somewhere else and photograph again: they're recognised (not new).
5. With the coin next to the pieces, take two more photos: the coin never shows up as a piece.

### M5 part 1: the live view
1. Under Pieces tap **📷 Live view of my table**. Allow the camera if asked.
2. Hold the phone about as high as for your scan. Within a second or two the line at the top should say **Tracking ✓ · N pieces in view**, and outlines appear on your pieces.
3. Move the phone slowly: left and right, turn it, a little higher and lower. Do the outlines stay on the pieces? While moving they may trail behind by a few millimetres; when you stop they should sit right on the pieces.
4. Green pieces carry a label like **26·1**. Tap a piece: a box at the bottom says what the app knows about it. Tap **Point it out**: it pulses. Move the phone away from it: an arrow at the screen's edge shows the way back.
5. Point the camera away from the pieces: the top line says **Looking for your pieces** and the outlines go. Point back: they come back.
6. Close the live view (**‹ Back**). Open **🎯 Fill this spot**, tap one of the holes, then **📷 Find it live**: the possible pieces for that hole pulse (green = the best one, yellow = also possible).

### M5 part 2: Fits / Doesn't fit
1. Under Pieces, tap a piece with a green dot. Under its spot it now asks **"Tried it in column X, row Y?"** with **✓ Fits** and **✗ Doesn't fit**.
2. Try that piece in that hole on your real puzzle.
   - It fits: tap **✓ Fits**. A green note says **"In the puzzle at column X, row Y"** with **Undo**; the piece leaves the list, and the line under the piece groups ends with **"✓ 1 in the puzzle"**.
   - It doesn't: tap **✗ Doesn't fit**. Its next best spot shows (or "You said it doesn't fit any of its suggested spots" with **Ask again**).
3. Tap **Undo** once: the piece comes back to the list. Then tap it and **✓ Fits** again. Tap **✓ 1 in the puzzle** (end of the line under the piece groups): the list shows it with **Take it out**.
4. If your scan counted the bottle label or a patch of table as a piece: tap it, then **Not a puzzle piece? Remove it** (twice to confirm).
5. In **🎯 Fill this spot**, tap a hole: each piece offered has **✓ Fits** and **✗ No**. After ✓ Fits the hole turns green on the picture and is no longer offered.
6. In **📷 Live view of my table**, tap a piece: the box at the bottom asks **"Tried it in column X, row Y?"** with the same two buttons.
7. Go on with as many pieces as you can (ideally all your holes): the more answers, the more the Accuracy page tells. A piece that doesn't fit any spot it was offered is then offered the other empty holes.
8. Tap **📊 How often is the app right?** (on the puzzle page, or in Settings): for Strong, Likely and Several, how many of its first choices were right, with a range that narrows as you answer more.

## M7 part 1: a new batch (do this last)

3. Take an overview photo: it should say **"Found N pieces (N new)"**, with no "could not be matched".
4. Back on the puzzle page, **Pieces read** says how many are on the table and set aside, and **Batch 2**.
5. Open **📷 Live view of my table**: only the new table's pieces are outlined.

## Good to know
- Pairs only appear when the app knows where both pieces go. A piece whose spot isn't sure gets no pairs yet; a close-up helps.
- Among your holes, pairs are the holes next to each other: around the group of 11 holes (columns 17–20, rows 7–9) and the 10 in the top-right corner (columns 24–28, rows 1–2).
- Live view, tested on the Mac with your own photos: on loose pieces the outlines sat within a fraction of a millimetre. Over the finished or half-built puzzle the repeating seams can make the outlines jump by a piece now and then. Hold the phone fairly flat: a very steep angle (like your IMG_9227) isn't recognised.
- ✓ Fits puts the piece into the puzzle in the app: it isn't outlined on your table any more. Tapped by mistake? **Undo** puts it back on the table, or back into its tray.
- After a piece fits, the holes next to it only get pieces whose knob or hole matches it (when the app has read both shapes clearly).
- Your answers are kept with the puzzle, and in backups.
