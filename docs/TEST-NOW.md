# M7, parts 1–2: a new batch, and the progress page (and M6, M5, still to check)

**New in M7 part 2: 📈 Progress** on the puzzle page (next to 📊 How often is the app right?). It shows how many pieces are in the puzzle, the box picture filled in (bright = done, a **yellow dot** = the piece for that spot is on your table or in a tray, dark = still to find), the pieces by where they are, corners and edges left, how often the app was right, and the batch. I tested it in the app with your photos: **987 of 1008 in the puzzle (98 %)**, and 17 of your 21 holes have their piece on the table.

**New in M7 part 1:** on your puzzle's page, under Pieces, **🔄 Start a new batch…**. Before, once you swept the pieces together (or put them back in the box) and spread out a new lot, no photo could be matched to the old table any more. Now a new batch sets the table's pieces aside (kept in the list), leaves the pieces in the puzzle and in trays where they are, and the next photo starts a fresh table.

**Good to know:** pieces you spread out again are read as new pieces. I tried recognising them by their picture and measured it first: on computer-made 300-piece batches, 3.5–8.7 % of brand-new pieces would have been mixed up with old ones, so it's off. If there's room, leave the leftovers where they are, spread the new pieces around them and scan as usual: then every piece is recognised by where it lies.

**I tested it myself** with a simulated camera in Safari's engine: the re-spread table wasn't matched, then after **Start a new batch** the next photo read all the pieces, and the counts showed them on the table and set aside.

Allow about 5 minutes for the progress page, 15 minutes for M6, 45 minutes for the M5 steps if you haven't done them yet, and 10 minutes for M7 part 1 (last). These steps are for version **{version}** (Settings shows the version).

## M6: which pieces join (still to check)
1. Under Pieces tap **🧩 Which pieces join**. The first card, **What next**, should be a **square of 4** (or a pair), with its spots marked on a small piece of the box picture.
2. Tap **📷 Point them out**: the camera opens and those pieces pulse, with a line between the ones that join. Find them on your table.
3. Try them together.
   - They fit: tap **✓ All 4 fit** (or **✓ They fit** for a pair). A green note says they're joined, with **Undo**, and the next card moves up.
   - A pair doesn't fit: tap **✗ Don't fit** on that pair. It isn't suggested again.
4. Go on with a few more cards. Near the bottom, **✓ N pairs joined** lists what you joined, each with **Take back**.
5. Close the panel and tap a piece with a green dot: under its spot, **Joins (as on the box picture)** shows its partners ("on its right · 95 %"), and which sides are next to the done part of the puzzle or have a partner not scanned yet.
6. Open **📷 Live view of my table**: pieces that probably join have a line between them (dashed white with a %, or green ✓ for a square of 4).
7. Tap **📊 How often is the app right?**: a new part **Which pieces join** counts your answers.

## Still to check from M5
**M5 in short:** the 📷 live view of your table (part 1), ✓ Fits / ✗ Doesn't fit with the 📊 page (part 2), rescans that don't count a piece twice (part 3), and a smoother live view that notices moved pieces, plus Quick scans (part 4).

### M5 part 4: a smoother live view, and Quick scans
1. Open **📷 Live view of my table** and move the phone steadily across the table: the outlines should keep up with the pieces instead of trailing behind.
2. Hold the phone still: the outlines should sit calmly on the pieces (no jitter).
3. With the live view open, take **2 pieces** off the table: within a second or two their outlines turn **red and dashed** ("moved?" on bigger pieces), and the top line says **"2 not where they were: scan again to update"**. Put them back where they were: the outlines turn normal again.
4. Open **Settings → Scan mode** and choose **Quick**. Scan your pieces: after one overview photo the note says **"Quick scan done"** and the camera stays on overviews. Set it back to **Full** afterwards (or keep Quick if you like it).

### M5 part 3: rescans and moved pieces
1. With your pieces scanned (as above), take another **overview photo** from a different place (Scan pieces). The note should say **"Found N pieces (0 new)"** (the coin and other things on the table aren't pieces).
2. Move **3 pieces** by hand to other places on the table (turn one of them round), then take another overview photo. The note should say **"3 pieces were moved: recognised, not counted again"**, or some of them become **pieces to check**.
3. If the puzzle page shows **"Pieces to check"**: tap **Check them**. Each row shows the piece as seen now and the pieces it may be: tap **This one** under the right one (or **A different piece**). The **?** goes away.
4. Take **2 pieces** off the table and photograph again: the note says **"2 pieces are no longer where they were"**. Put them back somewhere else and photograph again: they're recognised (not new).
5. With the coin next to the pieces, take two more photos: the coin never shows up as a piece.

### M5 part 1: the live view
1. Under Pieces tap **📷 Live view of my table**. Allow the camera if asked.
2. Hold the phone about as high as for your scan. Within a second or two the line at the top should say **Tracking ✓ · N pieces in view**, and outlines appear on your pieces.
3. Move the phone slowly: left and right, turn it, a little higher and lower. Do the outlines stay on the pieces? While moving they may trail behind by a few millimetres; when you stop they should sit right on the pieces.
4. Green pieces carry a label like **26·1**. Tap a piece: a box at the bottom says what the app knows about it. Tap **Point it out**: it pulses. Move the phone away from it: an arrow at the screen's edge shows the way back.
5. Point the camera away from the pieces: the top line says **Looking for your pieces** and the outlines go. Point back: they come back.
6. Close the live view (**‹ Back**). Open **🎯 Fill this spot**, tap one of the holes, then **📷 Find it live**: the possible pieces for that hole pulse (green = the best one, yellow = also possible).

### M5 part 2: Fits / Doesn't fit
1. Under Pieces, tap a piece with a green dot. Under its spot it now asks **"Tried it in column X, row Y?"** with **✓ Fits** and **✗ Doesn't fit**.
2. Try that piece in that hole on your real puzzle.
   - It fits: tap **✓ Fits**. A green note says **"In the puzzle at column X, row Y"** with **Undo**; the piece leaves the list, and the line under the piece groups ends with **"✓ 1 in the puzzle"**.
   - It doesn't: tap **✗ Doesn't fit**. Its next best spot shows (or "You said it doesn't fit any of its suggested spots" with **Ask again**).
3. Tap **Undo** once: the piece comes back to the list. Then tap it and **✓ Fits** again. Tap **✓ 1 in the puzzle** (end of the line under the piece groups): the list shows it with **Take it out**.
4. If your scan counted the bottle label or a patch of table as a piece: tap it, then **Not a puzzle piece? Remove it** (twice to confirm).
5. In **🎯 Fill this spot**, tap a hole: each piece offered has **✓ Fits** and **✗ No**. After ✓ Fits the hole turns green on the picture and is no longer offered.
6. In **📷 Live view of my table**, tap a piece: the box at the bottom asks **"Tried it in column X, row Y?"** with the same two buttons.
7. Go on with as many pieces as you can (ideally all your holes): the more answers, the more the Accuracy page tells. A piece that doesn't fit any spot it was offered is then offered the other empty holes.
8. Tap **📊 How often is the app right?** (on the puzzle page, or in Settings): for Strong, Likely and Several, how many of its first choices were right, with a range that narrows as you answer more.

## M7 part 1: a new batch (do this last)
**Do these steps last** (after M6 and M5): starting a new batch sets the pieces on your table aside, and the M6 and M5 steps need them on the table. Or try it on a spare puzzle (Home → New puzzle).
1. Have a few scanned pieces on the table. Sweep them together and spread them out again somewhere else. Open **Scan pieces** and take a photo: it should say **"This photo could not be matched to the pieces found so far"** (the problem the new batch solves).
2. Back on the puzzle page, under Pieces, tap **🔄 Start a new batch…**. Read the box, then tap **Start and scan**.
3. Take an overview photo: it should say **"Found N pieces (N new)"**, with no "could not be matched".
4. Back on the puzzle page, **Pieces read** says how many are on the table and set aside, and **Batch 2**.
5. Open **📷 Live view of my table**: only the new table's pieces are outlined.

## Good to know
- Pairs only appear when the app knows where both pieces go. A piece whose spot isn't sure gets no pairs yet; a close-up helps.
- Among your holes, pairs are the holes next to each other: around the group of 11 holes (columns 17–20, rows 7–9) and the 10 in the top-right corner (columns 24–28, rows 1–2).
- Live view, tested on the Mac with your own photos: on loose pieces the outlines sat within a fraction of a millimetre. Over the finished or half-built puzzle the repeating seams can make the outlines jump by a piece now and then. Hold the phone fairly flat: a very steep angle (like your IMG_9227) isn't recognised.
- ✓ Fits puts the piece into the puzzle in the app: it isn't outlined on your table any more. Tapped by mistake? **Undo** puts it back on the table, or back into its tray.
- After a piece fits, the holes next to it only get pieces whose knob or hole matches it (when the app has read both shapes clearly).
- Your answers are kept with the puzzle, and in backups.
