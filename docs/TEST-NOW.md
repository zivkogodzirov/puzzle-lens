# M9, part 5: the scan camera (and M9 parts 1–4, M8 parts 1, 3 and 4, M7, M6, M5, still to check)

**New in M9 part 5** (design stage 5 of 6): the scan camera is laid out like the iPhone Camera app: one instruction at the top, a round level in the middle, and at the bottom the last photo, a big shutter whose ring fills before a photo takes itself, and **Photos**. After a photo: a flash, a click and a tap you feel ("Sounds and taps" in Settings turns them off), named steps while it reads (with **Cancel** after 10 s), and a **results card** ("Found 22 pieces …") with **See where they go**, **Take another** and **See photo** (a zoomable photo).

**New in M9 part 4** (design stage 4 of 6): each puzzle now has **four tabs** at the bottom instead of one long page: **Table** (your table photo with its labels, the live view, a "Next step"), **Picture** (progress and the box picture as a map: tap a spot to see its pieces), **Pieces** (all your pieces, with Which pieces join and Sort into trays as pages of their own) and **More** (backup, new lot, scanned pieces, puzzle details, delete). **Scan** is the camera button at the top. Home shows each puzzle as a card with its picture and progress.

**Where things are now (for the older steps below):** Scan → the **camera button** at the top of a puzzle. Live view and Show my table → the **Table** tab. Fill a spot, Progress and "Photograph the puzzle so far" → the **Picture** tab. Which pieces join, Sort into trays, the filters and Pieces to check → the **Pieces** tab. Backup, Start a new batch (now "new lot of pieces"), Delete / Restore scanned pieces, rename, set-up and Delete this puzzle → the **More** tab. How often is the app right? → More (Tester mode).

**New in M9 part 3** (design stage 3 of 6): tapping a piece opens a **card from the bottom** with a close-up of the box picture and two big answers, **Not there** and **It fits**; every answer has an **Undo**; **"Not a piece"** is one tap with Undo. The words are new everywhere: "It fits / Not there", and "Very likely / Maybe" instead of Strong / Likely (percentages only in Tester mode). Outlines on photos and in the live view speak one language: **solid = fine, dashed = have a look** (with a small badge saying why), one label per piece that never overlap, a **?** button that explains it, and a **Pieces in view** list.

**New in M9 part 2** (design stage 2 of 6): the whole app now uses one set of building blocks: iPhone-style top bars whose **‹ back** goes to the screen you came from, lists, coloured message boxes, a sheet that slides up from the bottom to confirm big deletions, and a dark **Undo** message above the bottom bar. One set of line icons replaces the emoji. Nothing about how the app finds or places pieces changed.

**New in M9 part 1** (the first of 6 design stages): colours that are easy to read in light mode, bigger ✓ Fits / ✗ Doesn't fit buttons side by side, a dark edge under every piece outline so they show on your cream table, **Undo** in the live view, a **bar at the bottom of the puzzle page** (Scan · Live view · Fill a spot · Joins), the app reopening your last puzzle, the 🧪 button in the top bar, and a **Tester mode** switch in Settings (on for now, so nothing you test with disappears).

**New in M8 part 4:** the fixes from the app review. Bugs: the note after a scan photo no longer covers the gauge, a photo of the finished puzzle now says how many loose pieces lie beside it, a misread "finished" count no longer overwrites your saved empty spots, and a tapped piece's panel no longer jumps. Speed (on the Mac, with your own photos): scan photos take about a fifth less time, the first photo after **Start a new batch** no longer freezes for 2 seconds, working out where pieces go is 3–6 times faster, and trays sort in about 0.05 s instead of half a second. Each change was checked against the version before it by a second, independent check, and your 41 photos give the same pieces as before.

**New in M8 part 1:** once **12 pieces are in the puzzle at known spots** (✓ Fits), the app learns how your photos' colours map to the box picture and places every other piece again; the puzzle page says **"Colours learned from your N pieces in the puzzle"**. This is for a fresh puzzle on the whole picture (among the holes of a nearly finished puzzle it isn't needed). Measured on your 19 real pieces: right spot first **1 → 6**, in the top 5 **3 → 9**.

**New in the M8 part 3 follow-up:** a piece outlined much smaller than the others on a photo (usually a pale piece on your cream table) is marked **yellow, "only partly outlined"**, and its shape isn't used, only its picture. Your photo IMG_9127 has about 60 loose pieces (not 95, as I wrote first); all were found: 27 alone, about 29 in groups of touching pieces (spread them apart), 1 pale one only in part.

**New in M8 part 3:** scans are faster (on the Mac your 18 MP photos now take 4–7 s for the first photo of a scan and under 2 s for the next ones at about the same height, instead of 7–12 s), and the live view looks at fewer frames while you hold the phone still, to save battery.

**Nothing to test for it on *The Kiss*:** it uses its holes, where the colours come from the holes already. You'll see it on your next puzzle, after 12 pieces have gone in with ✓ Fits. The steps below are the ones still open from M7, M6 and M5.

## M9 part 5: the scan camera
### The camera screen
1. Open your puzzle and tap the **camera button** at the top. At the top: ‹, a **gear**, 1× and the flask; under them one short instruction in a dark pill with a coloured dot.
2. The first time, a card asks to **allow the tilt sensor**: tap **Allow** and accept. A round level appears in the middle of the picture.
3. Tilt the phone: the instruction says "Hold the phone flat". Hold it flat over the pieces and keep still: the white ring around the big round button fills (green) and the photo **takes itself** when it is full.
4. **Whole table** / **Close-ups** above the big button: the chosen one is yellow and underlined.
5. Bottom-left: a small map of your pieces; tap it to see it large.
6. The **gear** opens "Coin and help": choose your coin there.
7. The photo button right of the big button opens your **photo library**.
8. If the camera pauses (e.g. after the photo library), a card in the middle says **Camera paused**: **Resume camera** works, and so does ‹.

### After a photo
1. Take a photo with the big button: a **white flash**, a **click** (silent switch off) and a light **tap** you feel.
2. While it reads, the picture freezes and darkens with **Reading photo · Finding pieces · Saving**.
3. A **card** slides up: "Found N pieces", how many are new, how many are ready, and anything to check (touching or faint pieces). Nothing is hidden behind the camera's buttons.
4. **See photo**: pinch, double-tap and drag the photo. Tap a dashed outline: it zooms in and offers **Retake / It's fine**. **Done** brings the card back.
5. **Take another**: the card goes in one tap and the big button is free again. While the card is open, no photo takes itself.
6. **See where they go**: the puzzle's Table tab opens.
7. Photograph your nearly finished puzzle (or choose IMG_9280): "Finished puzzle: 28 × 36", with the saved empty spots and the loose pieces readable without scrolling.
8. Settings → **Sounds and taps** → Off: no flash, click or tap. (Put it back On if you like it.)
9. Does the click play and is the tap felt on your iPhone, also when a photo takes itself?

### Everywhere
1. **‹ Home** (or swiping back) from any tab goes straight to your puzzles, not to the previous tab.
2. Close the app fully and open it again: it reopens your puzzle on **Table**.
3. Nothing hides under the tab bar at the bottom of any tab.

## M9 part 3: tap a piece, answer, undo
### The piece card
1. Open *The Kiss* and tap a piece. A card slides up from the bottom: the piece's picture, **"Column … · Row …"**, a word like **Very likely** or **Maybe**, and a **close-up of the box picture** with the spot in a green square and the rest darker. Does the close-up help you find the spot on your puzzle?
2. Tap **Whole picture**: the whole box picture shows. Tap **Close-up** to go back. Swipe the card up: it grows; down: it shrinks or closes.
3. Tap **Not there**: the card moves straight to the next place to try, and "Not at …" with **Undo** appears above the card. Tap Undo: the first place comes back.
4. Tap **It fits**: the card closes and "Placed at …" with **Undo** shows. Tap Undo: the piece is back among your pieces.
5. Under "Also possible", tap one of the small pictures: the card shows that place instead.
6. Tap **Point it out**: the live view shows that one piece pulsing. Tap back: the card is there again.
7. Tap **Not a piece** once (on something that really isn't a piece, or tap **Undo** right away): it disappears with "Removed" and **Undo**. Undo brings it back.
8. **Fill a spot**: tap a spot, then a suggested piece's picture: its card opens on that spot. The buttons say **It fits** / **Not there**. **Joins**: "They fit" / "Not there".
9. On the puzzle page the summary now reads "N very likely · N maybe · N where several places look alike".

### Outlines on photos and in the live view
1. Open **Live view** and point the phone at your pieces. Pieces with a known spot have a small dark label like **17·9**; no two labels sit on top of each other.
2. The first time, a note says what "17·9" means. Tap **Got it**; it doesn't come back.
3. **Dashed** outlines always have a small yellow badge: **?** = check the piece is whole (a faint or part outline); **arrows apart** = these pieces touch, spread them apart; **moved?** = it isn't where it was.
4. Tap the **?** button at the top: a card explains the colours and badges.
5. Tap **Pieces in view**: a list of the outlined pieces; tap one and its box opens with **It fits** / **Not there**.
6. **Joins** → "What next" → **Point them out** on a square of 4: the four pieces are one green shape with one label.
7. **Show my table** and the last scan photo (**Scan** → **See the last photo**) use the same outlines, badges and **?**; anything that isn't a piece has a **cross**.
8. Does the live view still feel smooth?

### M5 part 1: the live view
1. Under Pieces tap **📷 Live view of my table**. Allow the camera if asked.
2. Hold the phone about as high as for your scan. Within a second or two the line at the top should say **Tracking ✓ · N pieces in view**, and outlines appear on your pieces.
3. Move the phone slowly: left and right, turn it, a little higher and lower. Do the outlines stay on the pieces? While moving they may trail behind by a few millimetres; when you stop they should sit right on the pieces.
4. Green pieces carry a label like **26·1**. Tap a piece: a box at the bottom says what the app knows about it. Tap **Point it out**: it pulses. Move the phone away from it: an arrow at the screen's edge shows the way back.
5. Point the camera away from the pieces: the top line says **Looking for your pieces** and the outlines go. Point back: they come back.
6. Close the live view (**‹ Back**). Open **🎯 Fill this spot**, tap one of the holes, then **📷 Find it live**: the possible pieces for that hole pulse (green = the best one, yellow = also possible).

### M5 part 2: Fits / Doesn't fit
1. Under Pieces, tap a piece with a green dot. Under its spot it now asks **"Tried it in column X, row Y?"** with **✓ Fits** and **✗ Doesn't fit**.
2. Try that piece in that hole on your real puzzle.
   - It fits: tap **✓ Fits**. A green note says **"In the puzzle at column X, row Y"** with **Undo**; the piece leaves the list, and the line under the piece groups ends with **"✓ 1 in the puzzle"**.
   - It doesn't: tap **✗ Doesn't fit**. Its next best spot shows (or "You said it doesn't fit any of its suggested spots" with **Ask again**).
3. Tap **Undo** once: the piece comes back to the list. Then tap it and **✓ Fits** again. Tap **✓ 1 in the puzzle** (end of the line under the piece groups): the list shows it with **Take it out**.
4. If your scan counted the bottle label or a patch of table as a piece: tap it, then **Not a puzzle piece? Remove it** (twice to confirm).
5. In **🎯 Fill this spot**, tap a hole: each piece offered has **✓ Fits** and **✗ No**. After ✓ Fits the hole turns green on the picture and is no longer offered.
6. In **📷 Live view of my table**, tap a piece: the box at the bottom asks **"Tried it in column X, row Y?"** with the same two buttons.
7. Go on with as many pieces as you can (ideally all your holes): the more answers, the more the Accuracy page tells. A piece that doesn't fit any spot it was offered is then offered the other empty holes.
8. Tap **📊 How often is the app right?** (on the puzzle page, or in Settings): for Strong, Likely and Several, how many of its first choices were right, with a range that narrows as you answer more.

## M7 part 1: a new batch (do this last)
**Do these steps last** (after M6 and M5): starting a new batch sets the pieces on your table aside, and the M6 and M5 steps need them on the table. Or try it on a spare puzzle (Home → New puzzle).
1. Have a few scanned pieces on the table. Sweep them together and spread them out again somewhere else. Open **Scan pieces** and take a photo: it should say **"This photo could not be matched to the pieces found so far"** (the problem the new batch solves).
2. Back on the puzzle page, under Pieces, tap **🔄 Start a new batch…**. Read the box, then tap **Start and scan**.
3. Take an overview photo: it should say **"Found N pieces (N new)"**, with no "could not be matched".
4. Back on the puzzle page, **Pieces read** says how many are on the table and set aside, and **Batch 2**.
5. Open **📷 Live view of my table**: only the new table's pieces are outlined.

## Good to know
- Pairs only appear when the app knows where both pieces go. A piece whose spot isn't sure gets no pairs yet; a close-up helps.
- Among your holes, pairs are the holes next to each other: around the group of 11 holes (columns 17–20, rows 7–9) and the 10 in the top-right corner (columns 24–28, rows 1–2).
- Live view, tested on the Mac with your own photos: on loose pieces the outlines sat within a fraction of a millimetre. Over the finished or half-built puzzle the repeating seams can make the outlines jump by a piece now and then. Hold the phone fairly flat: a very steep angle (like your IMG_9227) isn't recognised.
- ✓ Fits puts the piece into the puzzle in the app: it isn't outlined on your table any more. Tapped by mistake? **Undo** puts it back on the table, or back into its tray.
- After a piece fits, the holes next to it only get pieces whose knob or hole matches it (when the app has read both shapes clearly).
- Your answers are kept with the puzzle, and in backups.
