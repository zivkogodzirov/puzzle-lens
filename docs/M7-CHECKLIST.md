# M7 checklist for your iPhone: working in batches

The steps for the newest part are also inside the app: tap the small **🧪 Test** tab on the right edge of any screen.

## Part 1: a new batch

**New:** on your puzzle's page, under Pieces, **🔄 Start a new batch…**. Use it when you've put the pieces back in the box (or swept them together) and spread out a new lot. The pieces on the table are set aside (kept in the list), pieces in the puzzle and in trays stay where they are, and the next photo starts a fresh table.

**Good to know:** pieces you spread out again are read as new pieces. The app can't safely tell hundreds of pieces apart by their picture alone (I measured it: it would have mixed new pieces up with old ones). If there's room, it's easier to leave the leftovers where they are, spread the new pieces around them and scan as usual: then every piece is recognised by where it lies, and you don't need a new batch.

Allow about 10 minutes. Settings should show the version I sent you.

### Get ready
**Do these steps after the M6 and M5 checks** (or on a spare puzzle): a new batch sets the pieces on your table aside, and those checks need them on the table.
1. If a banner says a new version is ready, tap **Reload** first, then open this 🧪 Test tab again.
2. Have a few pieces on the table that the app has scanned (any puzzle).

### Check the new batch
1. Sweep the pieces together and spread them out again somewhere else on the table. Open **Scan pieces** and take a photo: it should say **"This photo could not be matched to the pieces found so far"**. (This is the problem the new batch solves.)
2. Go back to the puzzle page. Under Pieces, tap **🔄 Start a new batch…**. Read the box, then tap **Start and scan**.
3. Take an overview photo: it should say **"Found N pieces (N new)"**, with no "could not be matched".
4. Back on the puzzle page, **Pieces read** should say how many are on the table and how many are set aside, and **Batch 2**.
5. Open **📷 Live view of my table**: only the new table's pieces are outlined.

## Part 2: the progress page

**New:** **📈 Progress** on the puzzle page. It shows how many pieces are in the puzzle, the box picture filled in (bright = done, a yellow dot = the piece for that spot is on your table or in a tray, dark = still to find), the pieces by where they are, corners and edges left, how often the app was right, and the batch.

1. On *The Kiss*, tap **📈 Progress**. It should say about **987 of 1008 pieces in the puzzle**, from your photo of the puzzle.
2. On the box picture, your holes are dark; most have a yellow dot. Does that match your table?
3. Put a piece in (✓ Fits), then open Progress again: one more in, one dot fewer.
